Privacy Policy

Baccarat Gladiator · Effective 2026-05-09
⚠ Draft policy. Review with counsel before public launch — this text is a faithful description of the app's actual data flow, not a legal document.

Baccarat Gladiator is a single-player simulated baccarat game with no real-money wagering. This page explains what data we collect, why, and how it's stored.

What we collect

  • Account credentials. When you sign up, AWS Cognito stores your email address and a password hash. We never see or store your plaintext password.
  • High scores and gameplay stats. When you submit a score, we store the result, the stage you played, and your account ID so the leaderboard can attribute your entry.
  • Tournament submissions. If you participate in a monthly tournament, we store your bet history for the tournament shoe so the server can replay and verify your final balance.
  • Local app state. Sound preferences, the cleared- tier list, and audio toggles are stored locally on your device via the iOS UserDefaults / web localStorage. They never leave your device.

What we don't collect

  • No advertising identifiers (no IDFA, no Google Advertising ID).
  • No location data.
  • No camera, microphone, or photo-library access.
  • No third-party analytics SDKs (no Google Analytics, no Firebase Analytics, no Mixpanel, no Segment, etc.).
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking.
  • No real-money payment information — the app does not process payments.

Where data is stored

Account credentials are stored in AWS Cognito (US East 1 region). Scores and tournament submissions are stored in AWS DynamoDB and served via AWS Lambda. We do not share your data with any third party other than these AWS infrastructure services.

Children

Baccarat Gladiator is age-rated for simulated gambling and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone below the app's rated age. If you believe an under-age user has signed up, contact us and we'll delete the account.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask for corrections, or request full deletion of your account by emailing frankie@styleiq.co. We respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page and notify active accounts at next sign-in.
