What Baccarat Gladiator is — and is not.
Baccarat Gladiator is a free single-player strategy card-battle saga. Players use baccarat as the combat mechanic to clear 60 themed stages. Practice or simulated success in this game does not imply, predict, or correlate with success at real-money gambling.

Our commitments

  • No real-money gambling. No real cash, prizes, cryptocurrency, NFTs, sweepstakes coins, or items of monetary value are offered, awarded, or transferable through the game.
  • No cash-out. Virtual chips earned or purchased in the game cannot be exchanged for money, refunded as cash, or transferred to another player. They are entertainment credits with no value outside the app.
  • No player-to-player transfers. Players cannot send chips, items, or any in-game balance to each other. This rules out secondary markets entirely.
  • Age-gated. The app is age-rated and includes an age confirmation step on first launch. We do not knowingly accept users below the rated age.
  • Transparent monetization. Optional in-app purchases buy additional virtual chips for use within the game only. Purchases are processed by Apple, Google, or PayPal — never by us directly — and are clearly disclosed on the App Store listing and at the point of purchase.
  • Optional, capped advertising. Rewarded video ads are always opt-in, never forced, and capped per session so the experience remains a game rather than an ad stream.

Healthy-play guidance

  • Treat the game as entertainment, not as a training tool for real-money gambling — even though baccarat is a real casino game, simulated outcomes do not translate to real-money expected value.
  • Set a time budget before you start a session. Most adults find 20–40 minutes a healthy upper bound for any single play session.
  • Take a break after every cleared stage. The game is designed to be paused mid-progression at any time without losing progress.
  • Be honest about why you're playing. If you find yourself opening the app to chase a loss, regulate stress, or fill emotional voids, that is a signal to step away — even from a free-to-play, no-money game.
  • Talk to a friend or a professional if play stops feeling optional. Resources are listed below.

How to self-limit inside the app

  • Delete your local progress. Clearing the app's data on your device removes the session, the cleared-tier list, and the local chip balance. On iOS: Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Baccarat Gladiator → Offload App or Delete App.
  • Disable in-app purchases. On iOS: Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → In-App Purchases → Don't Allow. On Android: Google Play → Settings → Authentication.
  • Delete your account permanently. Email frankie@styleiq.co and we'll remove your account, scores, and any stored tournament history within 30 days.
